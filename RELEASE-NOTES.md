Flexible Price Coupons 1.2.0

Generalizes the former one-riyal plugin with admin-defined target prices and a saved currency per coupon. Includes native restrictions, usage limits, stacking protection, authorization and nonce checks for currency saving.

Deactivate Blue One Riyal Coupons before activating this plugin. Legacy coupons retain SAR 1 behavior. Choose Target product price to configure a new price and currency.

Currency selection restricts eligibility; it does not perform conversion. Tax follows store settings; shipping and fees are separate. WordPress.org submission and approval remain separate from public source publication.
